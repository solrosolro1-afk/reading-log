## 1. What exactly is BIQS? Definition, document numbers, ownership, and the "Built-In Quality" philosophy

### Takeaway
BIQS ("Built-In Quality Supply", sometimes written "Built-in Quality Supply Base" or "Built In Quality Supplier") is the GM Global Supplier Quality program that replaced QSB in 2016. It started as two things: a 29–30-element manufacturing-process standard (assessed with GM 1927 30 and taught through GM 1927 36), and a performance-based "BIQS Level" from 0 to 5. **GM 1927 03 is not the BIQS document.** GM 1927 03 (CG4338) is the *Supplier Quality Statement of Requirements*. BIQS sits under the GM 1927 Global Supplier Quality Manual family, and it is the supplier-side counterpart of "Built-in Quality", one of the five principles of GM's Global Manufacturing System (GMS).

### Cited Findings
**Document numbers (GM 1927 family)**
- [A] The GM CSR tells organizations to "refer to the CG4338 GM 1927 03 Supplier Quality Statement of Requirements (SOR) for requirements for organizations supplying parts and materials to General Motors." So GM 1927 03 is the SOR, which suppliers sign to acknowledge, not the BIQS standard. One copy says suppliers are expected to supply GM "with zero defects." — [GM CSR Jan 2025 (IATF-hosted)](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/IATF-16949-GM-Customer-Specific-Requirements-January-2025.pdf); [C] mirror of [CG4338 GM 1927 03 SOR](https://dokumen.tips/documents/cg4338-gm-1927-03-supplier-quality-statement-of-requirements.html)
- [C] **GM 1927 30** is the "BIQS Supplier Self-Assessment". The latest revision found is **rev. 15.0 (2020)**, as an .xlsx. A 2019 version also circulates. — [Scribd: GM 1927 30 BIQS Supplier SelfAssessment rev 15.0 2020](https://www.scribd.com/document/466005290/GM-1927-30-BIQS-Supplier-SelfAssessment-rev-15-0-2020-xlsx); [CourseHero copy](https://www.coursehero.com/file/193450619/GM-1927-30-BIQS-Supplier-SelfAssessment-rev-150-2020xlsx/); [PDFCoffee: GM 1927 30 2019](https://pdfcoffee.com/gm-1927-30-biqs-assessment-self-assessment-2019-pdf-free.html)
- [C] **GM 1927 36** is the BIQS training material. It is split into five independent modules: "Introduction and Foundational Elements" plus Groups A, B, C and D. A later copy is titled "GM 1927 36 QUALITY INCORPORATED IN MANUFACTURING (BIQS) INTRODUCTION 2019". — [Scribd: GM 1927 36 Introduction & Foundational Elements](https://www.scribd.com/document/481967216/GM-1927-36-Introduction-and-Foundational-Elements-of-BIQS); [Scribd: 1927 36 BIQS Introduction 2019](https://www.scribd.com/document/822410780/GM-1927-36-QUALITY-INCORPORATED-IN-MANUFACTURING-BIQS-INTRODUCTION-2019); [Scribd: Group A](https://www.scribd.com/document/555139077/GM-1927-36-Group-a-Elements); [Group C](https://www.scribd.com/document/555138644/GM-1927-36-Group-C-Elements-BIQS); [Group D](https://www.scribd.com/document/555138499/GM-1927-36-Group-D-Elements-BIQS)
- [C] **GM 1927** is the GM Global Supplier Quality Manual (SQM). Copies circulate as "2018 Rev 21" and "2020 Rev 27.0". — [PDFCoffee SQM 2018 Rev21](https://pdfcoffee.com/gm-1927-supplier-quality-manual-2018-rev21-pdf-free.html); [PDFCoffee SQM 2020 Rev 27.0](https://pdfcoffee.com/download/0-gm-1927-supplier-quality-manual-2020-rev-270-pdf-free.html)
- [B] Other GM 1927-xx numbers that appear in the CSR context: **GM 1927 70** "SQ IATF 16949 Certification Waiver", which replaced **GM 1927 71** "Quad Report" in Aug 2023 ([simpleQuE, Aug 2023](https://www.simpleque.com/how-the-latest-gm-iatf-16949-csr-revision-impacts-suppliers-as-of-august-31-2023/)). A search summary also named **GM 1927 17** as a sourceability-requirements document in the SupplyPower Document Library. That one comes only from an AI search summary and is unverified (grade C).

**BIQS content (elements)**
- [C] Group A elements: PFMEA, process capability, visual standards and controls, error proofing, bypass management, gauge calibration and MSA, process control plan. — [Scribd: GM 1927 36 Group A](https://www.scribd.com/document/555139077/GM-1927-36-Group-a-Elements)
- [C] Group D elements: reverse PFMEA, contamination control, FIFO material flow, approved packaging. — [Scribd: GM 1927 36 Group D](https://www.scribd.com/document/555138499/GM-1927-36-Group-D-Elements-BIQS)
- [C] The "BIQS House" model rests on foundational elements: "Supply Chain Management, Maintenance, and Training". — [Scribd: GM 1927 36 Intro](https://www.scribd.com/document/481967216/GM-1927-36-Introduction-and-Foundational-Elements-of-BIQS)
- [C] Other elements cited in BIQS overviews: change control, layered process audits (LPA), and shift-level monitoring of high-risk items (described as "BIQS-10"). — [search summary of ease.io 2016 article](https://www.ease.io/blog/three-key-gm-biqs-controls-every-organization-should-implement/); element list per [Scribd 1927 36 Group C](https://www.scribd.com/document/555138644/GM-1927-36-Group-C-Elements-BIQS)
- [C] The GM 1927 30 self-assessment rates each element compliant, noncompliant or N/A, with color maturity levels. Red = "the quality system or process is not evident, or the current system or process in place puts GM at significant risk". Green = a mature, well-defined system that is followed and does not put GM at unnecessary risk. The form has a "BIQS Score" field. — [PDFCoffee: GM 1927 30 (2019)](https://pdfcoffee.com/gm-1927-30-biqs-assessment-self-assessment-2019-pdf-free.html)

**Name variants**
- [B] Sources expand the acronym differently: "Built-In Quality Supply", "Built in Quality Supply Base", "Built In Quality Supplier". One low-quality source even says "Business Integration Quality Score", which is wrong. — [ease.io (2018)](https://www.ease.io/blog/what-auto-suppliers-need-to-know-about-new-gm-biqs/); [Scribd 2020 BIQS levels](https://www.scribd.com/document/466005233/2020-Quality-Performance-Requirements-BIQS-Levels-1-pdf)

**"Built-In Quality" philosophy (GMS origin)**
- [B] A 2002 GM release describes the Global Manufacturing System (GMS) as built on five principles: **People Involvement, Standardization, Built-in Quality, Short Lead Time, Continuous Improvement**. It says they work together as one system aimed at safety, people, quality, responsiveness and cost. — [The Auto Channel (GM release, 2002-01-09)](https://www2020.theautochannel.com/news/2002/01/09/034330.html)
- [B] Under GMS, GM moved PFMEA from engineering "onto the factory floor" so defects would not pass from station to station. The Andon system is meant to ensure "quality is built in at each station". — [IndustryWeek: "Manufacturing Strategy Plays a Key Role in GM's Revival"](https://www.industryweek.com/innovation/process-improvement/article/21957976/manufacturing-strategy-plays-a-key-role-in-gms-revival); [WardsAuto: GM Manufacturing Starting to Fire on All Cylinders](https://wardsauto.com/general-motors/gm-manufacturing-starting-to-fire-on-all-cylinders)
- [B] GM runs a parallel **BIQ (Built-In Quality) certification for its own and JV plants**, at levels BIQ I–IV. Examples: GM Uzbekistan / UzAuto Motors' Asaka plant received "BIQ III" in October 2020, and UzAuto Motors later "re-confirmed" BIQ IV. — [Kun.uz, 2020-10-10](https://kun.uz/en/news/2020/10/10/asaka-plant-receives-gm-quality-certificate); [UzDaily: GM Powertrain Uzbekistan BIQ III](https://www.uzdaily.uz/en/gm-powertrain-uzbekistan-receives-third-level-of-quality-integration-to-production-process-biq-iii/); [UzAvtosanoat: UzAuto Motors BIQ IV](https://uzavtosanoat.uz/en/news_show/uzauto_motors_aj_biq_iv_sifat_standartlari_sertifikatini_yana_bir_bor_muvaffaqiyatli_tasdiqladi_jarayonlar_yutuqlar_va_kelajakdagi_rejalar1737310593)

**Ownership**
- [A/B] The GM CSR and the BIQS documents are issued by "General Motors Global Supplier Quality". That is the title of the 2016 GM CSR, a function within GM Global Purchasing & Supply Chain. Suppliers reach the documents through GM SupplyPower's Document Library (Supplier Quality section). — [GM CSR for ISO/TS 16949, 26 Oct 2016](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/GM-Customer-Specifics-Requirements-ISO-TS-16949-26Oct2016-1.pdf)

### Inferences
- The prompt's "GM 1927 03 BIQS" label conflates two documents. A Korean supplier should be told: **GM 1927 03 = SQ Statement of Requirements (signed contract-level document)**, **GM 1927 30 = BIQS self-assessment**, **GM 1927 36 = BIQS training modules**, **GM 1927 = SQ Manual**.
- BIQS is best explained as GMS "Built-in Quality" pushed out to the supply base: detect at the station, contain, then prevent through PFMEA, error proofing and LPA. GM's own plants are certified under BIQ I–IV. Suppliers are measured under BIQS.

### Gaps
- No official GM definition sentence for BIQS could be read directly. SupplyPower is behind a login, and the IATF-hosted PDFs could not be fetched in this session.
- "GM 1927 30 = BIQS Level 1" (the prompt's guess) is not supported. GM 1927 30 is the self-assessment, not a level.
- GM 1927 17 (sourceability) is unverified.
- The element count differs by source: 29 vs 30 (see §2).

## 2. When and why BIQS was introduced, and how it evolved (version history to Nov 2026)

### Takeaway
GM started moving suppliers from **QSB (Quality Systems Basics, 11 elements, rev. 2006/2009)** to **BIQS (29–30 elements) in January 2016**. Training was restructured into five modules in 2018. The latest self-assessment found is rev 15.0 (2020). Around 2020 the on-site BIQS assessment/score was dropped in favor of IATF 16949 requirements, and the "BIQS Level" became a purely **performance-based** rating. **From 1 January 2023 the GM CSR renamed "BIQS Requirements" to "GM Quality Performance Requirements" and "BIQS Level" to "Sourceability Level"**, a level of 3, 4 or 5 still required. Later CSR revisions (Aug 2023, Jan 2025, Mar 2025, Oct 2025) tightened the consequences. No 2026 GM CSR revision was found as of Nov 2026, yet Korean press in 2026 still says "BIQS 레벨 5".

### Cited Findings
**Predecessor: QSB**
- [C] GM's Quality Systems Basics (QSB) was revised in 2006, and material labeled "rev March 2009" circulates. A ~2009 forum post says suppliers were expected to complete QSB training for the 2010 model year. — [Elsmar: What is QSB Training? It's a GM thing](https://elsmar.com/elsmarqualityforum/threads/what-is-qsb-training-its-a-gm-thing-quality-system-basic-training.19614/); [Scribd: QSB presentation 2007](https://www.scribd.com/presentation/683587325/QSB-PRESENTACION-2007); [PDFCoffee: QSB tools overview](https://pdfcoffee.com/qsb-tools-gm-quality-system-basics-overview-pdf-free.html)

**Introduction of BIQS (2016)**
- [B] "In January 2016, GM began moving suppliers from QSB (11 elements) to Built in Quality Supply Base (29 elements)." Exceptions: BIQS Level 0 (no production receipts and no quality SPPS records in the last 12 months), or an organization not yet IATF-certified. — [GM CSR Dec 2020](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/IATF-16949-GM-CSR-Dec-2020.pdf); [GM Scorecard Quick Reference Guide, Aug 2020](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/GM-Scorecard-quick-reference-guide6Aug2020.pdf); [GM Scorecard Screen Images, Aug 2020](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/GM-Scorecard-screen-images_6Aug2020.pdf)

**Why GM introduced BIQS**
- [B] ease.io frames BIQS as GM's move from a narrow "basics" checklist (QSB) to a broader, more proactive manufacturing quality system, with LPA, PFMEA-based risk analysis and rapid response/problem solving with daily monitoring as core expectations. — [ease.io 2016](https://www.ease.io/blog/three-key-gm-biqs-controls-every-organization-should-implement/); [ease.io 2018](https://www.ease.io/blog/what-auto-suppliers-need-to-know-about-new-gm-biqs/)

### Inferences
- BIQS rolled out in January 2016, about two years after GM's February 2014 ignition-switch recall crisis, during GM's broader post-2014 quality overhaul. **No source found explicitly links BIQS to the ignition-switch crisis.** Present this as timing context only, not as a stated cause.
- In practice "BIQS" now means two things. (1) The **process standard** (the 29–30 elements, GM 1927 36 training, GM 1927 30 self-assessment). It still shapes how GM SQEs judge a supplier's manufacturing floor, but the separate BIQS audit score seems to have been folded into IATF 16949 + CSR auditing from about 2020 (grade C evidence). (2) The **performance level** (0–5), officially renamed "Sourceability Level" in Jan 2023 but still colloquially called "BIQS Level" by suppliers and Korean media. A "BIQS Level 5 guide" should explain both names.
- The direction of travel from 2023 to 2025 is clear: consequences tightened (performance complaint/decertification path, gap assessment per DUNS below L3, SPPS feeding CB audit planning, CS2 counted as a performance indicator).

## 3. Relationship with IATF 16949, the GM CSR, and GM's other systems (PPAP, GP-12, GQTS, SupplyPower, CS1/CS2, NBH, awards)

### Takeaway
BIQS / Sourceability Level is **written into the GM CSR for IATF 16949 (clause 9.1.2.1)**, so it is mandatory for IATF-certified GM suppliers, and IATF 16949 certification does **not** replace it. IATF 16949 is the QMS baseline. The GM CSR adds GM-specific obligations. The BIQS/Sourceability Level is the GM performance gate that, if missed, feeds straight back into the supplier's IATF certificate status through the CB. GP-12, Controlled Shipping (CS1/CS2), New Business Hold, SPPS and GQTS are the operational systems whose records drive that level.

### Cited Findings
- [A] The GM CSR (Dec 2020) states: "Organizations shall achieve and maintain BIQS Level of 3, 4 or 5." Below Level 3, notify the CB within 5 business days. The CB issues a **major nonconformance** for Level 1 or 2. — [GM CSR Dec 2020](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/IATF-16949-GM-CSR-Dec-2020.pdf); quoted on [Elsmar](https://elsmar.com/elsmarqualityforum/threads/received-a-major-finding-during-iatf-surveillance-audit-for-loss-of-biqs-level-3-more-than-6-spps-in-6-months-how-should-we-address-systemic-ca.77943/post-640895)
- [A] The GM CSR (Jan 2025) states: "Organizations shall achieve and maintain a Sourceability Level of 3, 4 or 5." Below 3 → a performance complaint, which can start the certificate decertification process. Exception: "Sourceability Level 0 – With no production receipts nor any quality SPPS records in the last 12 months." — [GM CSR Jan 2025](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/IATF-16949-GM-Customer-Specific-Requirements-January-2025.pdf)
- [A] Mar 2025: a QMS gap assessment per manufacturing DUNS with a Sourceability Level < 3 (§9.2.2.2). Oct 2025: SPPS considered in CB audit planning (§9.2.2.3). — [IATF 28 Feb 2025](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/?p=6039); [IATF 14 Oct 2025](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/news/14-october-2025-gm-customer-specific-requirements-update/)
- [A] **New Business Hold (NBH):** the CB "shall take the decision to place the organization on immediate suspension upon receiving notice of GM New Business Hold (NBH)". The organization notifies the CB within 5 business days and submits a corrective action plan to the CB and affected customers within 10 business days. In the 2020 edition the certificate is withdrawn if suspension isn't lifted within **110 calendar days** of the NBH-Quality notice. — [GM CSR Dec 2020](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/IATF-16949-GM-CSR-Dec-2020.pdf); [GM CSR Jan 2023](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/IATF-16949-GM-CSR-Jan-2023.pdf). The 110-day figure was not re-confirmed in the 2023/2025 editions.
- [A] **Controlled Shipping Level 2 (CS II):** notify the registrar/CB within 5 business days. Since Jan 2025, CS II is a "performance indicator" under 9.1.2.1, no longer a "special status". — [GM CSR Jan 2025](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/IATF-16949-GM-Customer-Specific-Requirements-January-2025.pdf); [IATF notice 23 Jan 2025](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/news/23-january-2025-gm-csr-update/)
- [B] **GP-12 (Early Production Containment)** "shall be performed as required during launch and until released by the organization's assigned SQE or designate". It applies to pre-production and production builds that require PPAP, and whenever GM mandates it for parts that are a significant risk to a customer plant. ### Inferences
- How to frame it for managers: **IATF 16949 (QMS baseline) + GM CSR (GM-specific "shall"s, incl. ## 4. Purpose and objectives of BIQS, and how the level affects sourcing

### Takeaway
BIQS exists to keep defects from reaching GM by making suppliers build quality into each station: PFMEA-driven risk reduction, error proofing, LPA, rapid response, change control. It also gives GM one harmonized global yardstick for supplier manufacturing quality. The resulting level now carries the explicit name **"Sourceability" Level**, which signals that it gates new-business eligibility. A Level of 3 or more is required to stay in good standing under the GM CSR and with the IATF certificate.

### Cited Findings
- [A/C] GM expects suppliers to deliver parts "with zero defects" (GM 1927 03 SOR context). — [mirror of CG4338 GM 1927 03](https://dokumen.tips/documents/cg4338-gm-1927-03-supplier-quality-statement-of-requirements.html)
- [B] BIQS requires LPA, process risk analysis through PFMEA, a rapid response and problem-solving process, and daily monitoring. It also requires shift-level checks of high-risk items. — [ease.io 2016](https://www.ease.io/blog/three-key-gm-biqs-controls-every-organization-should-implement/)
- [B] The rename "BIQS Level → Sourceability Level" (effective 1 Jan 2023) comes with a "Sourceability Report" on which the level is viewed. — [simpleQuE](https://www.simpleque.com/general-motors-revises-iatf-16949-customer-specific-requirements-effective-1-1-23/); [Isometric Consulting](https://isometricconsulting.com/2022/12/29/gm-releases-updated-iatf-16949-customer-specific-requirements-effective-january-1-2023/)
- [A] Below Level 3 → a performance complaint and possible decertification (Jan 2025), plus a QMS gap assessment per DUNS (Mar 2025). An NBH triggers immediate certificate suspension. — [GM CSR Jan 2025](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/IATF-16949-GM-Customer-Specific-Requirements-January-2025.pdf); [IATF 28 Feb 2025](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/?p=6039); [GM CSR Jan 2023](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/IATF-16949-GM-CSR-Jan-2023.pdf)
- [B] The level is driven by quality performance: SPPS counts, SPPS severity, disruptions, field actions, controlled shipping. "Red" status = any field action, more than 1 disruption, 6 or more SPPS, or SPPS severity > 6.75 (= BIQS Level 1 or 2). — [simpleQuE](https://www.simpleque.com/customer-scorecards-and-the-supplier-performance-initiative-spi-to-enhance-customer-satisfaction/); [Scribd 2020 BIQS levels](https://www.scribd.com/document/466005233/2020-Quality-Performance-Requirements-BIQS-Levels-1-pdf)
- [B] Korean press calls BIQS "GM이 협력사의 제조 품질과 공정 관리 역량을 평가하는 기준" (the standard GM uses to evaluate suppliers' manufacturing quality and process control capability) and Level 5 "GM 품질평가시스템 최고등급" (the top grade in GM's quality evaluation system). — [Herald K, 2026-06-01](https://www.heraldk.com/article/2026060117292669812); [MyDaily, 2026-06-02](https://www.mydaily.co.kr/page/view/2026060216274050226)

### Inferences
- **GM's view:** fewer defects and disruptions at GM plants, global standardization of supplier manufacturing controls, an objective input to sourcing, and a lever through the CB/IATF certificate.
- **Supplier's view:** Level 3 is the floor for keeping IATF/GM standing. Level 4–5 is the realistic competitive target for new business and for award eligibility (SOY/SQEA). Being "sourceable" is what matters commercially.

### Gaps
- No official GM text was found stating the exact sourcing rule, e.g. "only Level 4/5 DUNS are eligible for new business quotes", or how Level 3 suppliers are treated in sourcing. The rule is implied by the "Sourceability" name and the NBH mechanism, but should be confirmed with the GM SQE/buyer or the GM Quality Performance Requirements on SupplyPower.
- No direct GM executive quote on BIQS's purpose was found.
